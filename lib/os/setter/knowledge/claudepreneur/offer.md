## Offer
I help founders, operators and coaches build their first real AI agents and
workflows — hands-on, 1-on-1. It's not a course and not coaching: I literally
build the first agent alongside you over a few sessions so you stop doing
everything manually. Internally: the AI Business OS engagement.

## Outcome
They stop being the bottleneck. Content, outbound and delivery run on systems
they own, so they get their time back and the business keeps moving when they
step away.

## Price
never quoted in DMs, only on the call. It depends on where they are and what
they need, so it's better to figure that out live than throw a number in DMs.

## Mechanism
We build the first agent together, on their actual business, in a few working
sessions — so the system exists and runs before we're done, instead of a course
they'd have to implement alone.

## What it is NOT
Not a course. Not a coaching program. Not an agency that takes it away and
delivers a black box. Someone builds it WITH you.

## Guarantee
none

## ICP
Founders, operators and coaches stuck doing everything themselves — usually
$3k–$30k/mo, 6–24 months solo, drowning in content + outbound + delivery. They
know AI/automation could free them up but don't have the time or the system to
actually build it. Niche overlap to qualify on: AI workflows, agents, content
systems, automation, Claude Code, async ops.

## Who I don't serve
People with no business yet and no revenue (point them at the free resources).
Agencies, VAs or creators pitching their own service. Anyone looking for a
done-for-you build with zero involvement.

## Objections

### How much does it cost
Deflect warmly to the call — it depends on where they are and what they need, so
it's better to figure that out live than throw a number in DMs. Never quote price
in the DM.

### I'm not looking for coaching
It's not coaching or a course at all — it's hands-on, I build the first agents
alongside them for a few sessions. Reframe to "someone builds it with you."

### Can you just send me more info
The call walks them through it way better than text can; offer a quick 15 min.
If they insist on text, give a one-line what-you-do and leave the door open.

### I don't have time
It's a quick 15 min, and the whole point of what I build is giving them time
back — the system removes work, it isn't another thing on the pile.

### I want to build a SaaS / product, not a service
NOT a disqualification. Reframe the vehicle: most founders who shipped a real
product started hands-on first — that's how you learn what people actually need,
and it pays you (audience, content, trust, revenue) while you figure the product
out. Collapse to "let's get the first agents shipped + you to real revenue first,
then that funds the product build." Then bridge as normal.
